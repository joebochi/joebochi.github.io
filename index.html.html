The Challenge

Nairobi's Transport Crisis

Nairobi's public transport, dominated by Matatu SACCOs, faces systemic failures that hinder economic productivity, degrade quality of life, and intensify environmental damage. The city needs a transformative — not incremental — solution.

🚌

Inefficiency & Unreliability

Unpredictable routes, overcrowding, and no real-time tracking leave millions stranded daily.

💸

Fare Inconsistencies

Cash-only systems enable overcharging, lack financial transparency, and exclude digital users.

🌫️

Environmental Pollution

Ageing diesel fleets contribute heavily to Nairobi's deteriorating air quality and carbon footprint.

🏙️

CBD Gridlock

Uncoordinated operations clog the Central Business District, costing billions in lost productivity.
